How COVID-19
affects the body

A respiratory virus can enter through the airways, multiply in cells, and trigger an immune response. What happens next varies from person to person.

1 · Exposure Infectious particles can be inhaled, especially in shared indoor air.

What the virus does

It enters some airway cells and uses them to make copies. The immune system then works to control the infection.

Why symptoms vary

Age, health, immunity, and the infection itself all matter. Many people have mild illness; some develop serious lung or whole-body effects.
